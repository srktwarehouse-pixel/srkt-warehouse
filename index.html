Our Story

Three Generations.
One Standard.

1937
Founded in Rajahmundry as a transport company. Started moving raw tobacco on bullock carts. First movers in the region's logistics sector.
1950s – 80s
First in the region to run coal, petrol, and diesel trucks. Fleet of 65 vehicles. Routes to Bangalore, Kolkata, Bihar, UP, Pune, and the Nepal border.
1993
ITC invited us into 3PL warehousing. Built 3 lakh sqft near Rajahmundry. Ran the entire operation end-to-end.
2013
Expanded to Mysore with 2.4 lakh sqft. Retail 3PL model fully established. New clients including Cargill, Flipkart, and Delhivery.
2021 – Present
Active 3PL for HUL and Cargill. New services: Warehouse Advisory & Development and Warehouse Facilitation across AP and Telangana.
